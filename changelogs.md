# Changelog

## [1.6.6] - 2026-10-01

### Added

- Background playback that survives swipe-up, recents and screen-off
- Tap any card to open its recording
- Page transitions: Slide, Fade or Zoom in, under Appearance
- Press haptics with a strength slider, under Appearance → Gestures
- Transport buttons swell on tap while neighbors squeeze in
- Back while recording sends the app home instead of closing it

### Fixed

- Search bar no longer collapses from system gestures
- Update downloads no longer stall or crash
- Pages slide the full width instead of stopping halfway
- Folders can't be swiped away by accident
