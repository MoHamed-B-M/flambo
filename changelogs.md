# Changelog

## [1.4.0] - 2026-09-23

A small but meaningful update focused on making your recordings easier to manage and your daily use a bit smoother.

### What's new

- **Recording details in Playback** — Tap the new `ⓘ` button in the top bar to see where your file is saved, its size, date and duration in a clean card.
- **Swipe tip with a gentle bounce** — When you turn on *Swipe to dismiss*, a small preview card bounces to show you how it works.
- **Settings redesign** — A refreshed Settings UI with clearer sections, consistent cards and smoother navigation across Recording, Appearance, Speech-to-Text, Storage and Updates.
- **New color schemes** — Additional expressive palettes in Appearance let you personalize the app while keeping a clean, consistent look across light and dark themes.

### Fixes

- **File names now match what you see in the app** — New recordings are saved as "Recording 1", "Recording 2", etc., just as they appear in the list.
- **Deleted files are now really gone** — Clearing the trash or deleting permanently removes the file from both the app and your custom folder.
- **Custom folder stays remembered** — Picking a folder works reliably after updates or restarts.
- **About version is automatic** — The version in Settings > About now follows the app version, no more manual updates.

### Improvements

- Playback moved **inside each card** — No more top banner. Tap play on a card to listen, see the timer and waveform move right there. Only one recording plays at a time.
- Smoother scrolling and playback — Lists run more efficiently and the progress updates more gently.
- Swipe feels cleaner — No more black flash or delay when swiping back.
- Builds clean up after 7 days — Old preview builds are deleted automatically.
